What we do

Product Sourcing

We work directly with Italian producers — family-owned, region-specific brands producing in small batches with a strong sense of origin.

Export & Compliance

We manage documentation, labelling requirements and export readiness for the Australian market, reducing delays and compliance risk.

Logistics & Consolidation

We consolidate products across suppliers and regions, coordinating sea or air freight to simplify importing and control costs.

Marketing

We support brand launches into the Australian market and ongoing digital content to promote product usage and adoption.

Our approach prioritises quality, transparency, and long-term partnerships over volume or trend-driven products.
