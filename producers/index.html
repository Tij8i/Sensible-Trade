Export Your Products to Australia — With the Right Partner

We work with Italian food and specialty product producers who want to enter or expand in the Australian market without navigating export complexity alone.

Based in Italy and focused exclusively on Australia, we act as your commercial and export partner — representing your products with care, clarity, and long-term intent.

Who This Is For

This service is designed for Italian producers who:

  • Are family-owned or independent brands
  • Produce high-quality, authentic products in small to medium batches
  • Want selective, long-term market entry, not mass distribution
  • Value brand positioning as much as volume

We do not operate as a marketplace or bulk trader.

What We Do for Producers

Market Access

We introduce your products to suitable Australian buyers, including specialty retailers, hospitality groups, and importers aligned with your brand values.

Export & Compliance Support

We guide you through Australian export requirements, including:

  • Product documentation
  • Labelling expectations
  • Category-specific regulations (food, alcohol, non-food)

Logistics Coordination

We manage consolidated shipments, reducing freight costs and simplifying export logistics — particularly for producers shipping smaller volumes.

Brand Representation

Your products are presented accurately and professionally, with respect for origin, process, and positioning.

Why Work With Us

  • Italy-based, Italian-speaking partner
  • Direct knowledge of Australian import standards
  • Small-batch and niche-brand friendly
  • Transparent commercial structure
  • Focus on sustainable, repeat business — not one-off orders

We build measured, realistic pathways into the Australian market.

Product Categories We Currently Represent

  • Artisan pasta & baked goods
  • Olive oils, condiments & preserves
  • Boutique spirits & aperitivi
  • Specialty pantry products
  • Select Italian lifestyle & tabletop items

If your product sits outside these categories, we're still happy to discuss.

How the Collaboration Works

  1. Initial discussion about your products and export goals
  2. Market suitability assessment for Australia
  3. Commercial and logistics alignment
  4. Introduction to selected Australian buyers
  5. Ongoing export and relationship support
